Who will I see?

Dr Julia is the principal. She plans your care and does the complex treatment. A dentist can only see so many people — as the practice grows, some visits (hygiene, reviews, general) may be with clinicians she trusts. Continuity of the plan, not a one-chair ceiling.

How do I book?

Online booking is not live yet. Call [phone] once a number is published, or use the contact page.

Do you publish fees?

Not until Julia confirms ranges. You get a written plan with ADA item numbers after examination. See fees.

Are there risks with cosmetic or implant treatment?

Yes. Invasive and cosmetic work carries recovery and failure notes. Read treatment risks. Julia will assess what is appropriate — this site does not call you a candidate.
