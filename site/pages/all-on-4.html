All-on-4 Fixed Bridge vs. Removable Dentures

Living with failing teeth or loose dentures affects your confidence, digestion, and daily comfort. Here is how fixed implant bridges compare to conventional dentures:

Permanent Solution

All-on-4 Fixed Bridge

A full arch of handcrafted teeth screwed securely onto four to six titanium implants.

  • Chewing Power90–95% Natural Biting Force
  • Roof of Mouth (Palate)Completely Open (Taste & Feel)
  • StabilityFixed Solidly (Never Moves)
  • Bone HealthStimulates Bone & Prevents Shrinking
  • Night RoutineStays in Your Mouth

Cleaned like natural teeth with specialized floss and water flossers; never removed in a glass.

Traditional Dentures

A removable plastic acrylic plate resting on the gums and held by suction or paste.

  • Chewing Power20–30% Biting Force
  • Roof of Mouth (Palate)Covered with Thick Acrylic
  • StabilityCan Slip, Click, or Dislodge
  • Bone HealthAccelerates Jawbone Resorption
  • Night RoutineMust Be Removed Every Night

Requires periodic relining as gums and jawbone continue to shrink over the years.

The All-on-4 Treatment Journey

Full-arch rehabilitation is carefully planned in 3D before any surgery takes place. Here is the clinical workflow:

01

3D Planning & Virtual Guide

Comprehensive exam, 3D CBCT scans, and digital facial scanning to position implants into dense bone without sinus grafting.

02

Surgery & Immediate Teeth

Failing teeth removed, implants placed with surgical guides, and an immediate fixed bridge fitted so you never leave without teeth.

03

Bone Integration

3 to 6 months of bone healing while eating a modified soft diet as the implants integrate permanently into your jaw.

04

Final Zirconia Bridge

Custom-milled titanium-reinforced monolithic zirconia bridge fitted, balanced for bite dynamics, and sealed for life.

Surgical Setting & General Anaesthesia: While surgery can be performed under local anaesthetic with IV twilight sedation in our Wheelers Hill practice, patients preferring full unconscious general anaesthesia (GA) are scheduled through accredited private hospitals alongside specialist anaesthetists.

Honest Clinical Considerations & Risks (AHPRA s133)

All-on-4 is an extensive surgical rehabilitation. Under Australian health law, patients must understand the clinical risks involved:

  • Significant Post-Operative Swelling: Moderate to severe facial swelling, jaw tightness, and bruising are expected for 7 to 10 days post-surgery.
  • Implant Failure Risk: In rare cases, an individual implant may fail to osseointegrate, requiring removal, a healing period, and a replacement fixture.
  • Strict Soft Food Diet: You must strictly follow a soft chew diet (pasta, fish, soft vegetables) for 3 months to prevent micro-movements on healing implants.
  • Lifelong Maintenance: Bridges must be professionally unscrewed, cleaned, and checked at our practice annually to prevent peri-implant infection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I be left without teeth at any point?

No. An immediate fixed temporary bridge is attached to your implants within 24 to 48 hours of surgery, so you will leave with a fixed, functional set of teeth.

How long does the surgery take?

A single arch typically takes 2.5 to 3.5 hours of precision surgical placement under sedation. Both arches take approximately 5 hours.

How do I clean underneath an All-on-4 bridge?

We train you with specialized dental floss threaders (Superfloss) and a water flosser (Waterpik) to flush out food particles underneath the bridge daily.

Can early release of superannuation cover All-on-4?

Yes. Full-arch rehabilitation frequently qualifies under the ATO compassionate release program because severe tooth loss prevents adequate chewing and nutritional health. We supply the clinical paperwork.
