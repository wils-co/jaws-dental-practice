Dr Julia Nguyen is a general dentist with over fifteen years of experience in private practice.

She came to dentistry through science, completing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Medical Laboratory Science at RMIT in 2003 before graduating with a Bachelor of Dental Science from the University of Western Australia in 2009. She had also been offered an opportunity to get into medicine on the strength of her science results, and chose dentistry instead — drawn to a discipline that asked for clinical judgement with the artistry of making things with her hands.

Much of Julia's spare time goes into further training. She has completed a Prosthodontics Mini-Residency in full-mouth rehabilitation, the ARIA Master Implant Program, implant and bone grafting (GBR) training with gIDE in Los Angeles, Progressive Orthodontic Seminars, and Invisalign certification, just to name a few.

Although experienced across all areas of general dentistry, her focus is cosmetic and restorative work — implants, full-mouth rehabilitation, digital smile design, veneers and crowns, and complex treatment planning — along with cracked and worn teeth.

Julia sees teeth as a gateway to whole-body health, and aims to deliver considered and not routine care, that's shaped to her patients' needs.

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys cooking, gardening, and time with her children.
