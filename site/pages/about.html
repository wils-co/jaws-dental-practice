"Your Teeth Have a Timeline"

Dentistry is not a commodity, and mouths do not follow a generic assembly line. Every tooth in your mouth has a history and a future. When you shave away natural tooth enamel, that decision is permanent.

At Smiles by Design, Dr. Julia adheres to a strict Conservative First philosophy:

  • Preserve Natural Biology: If a tooth can be straightened with aligners or brightened with non-invasive whitening, we will always recommend that path before cutting tooth enamel for veneers.
  • Continuity of Care: In corporate group practices, patients often see a different dentist at every visit. Here, Dr. Julia personally examines your mouth, designs your plan, and performs your treatment.
  • No Production Quotas: Because this is an independent private practice, your care is never influenced by corporate sales targets or daily billing minimums.
