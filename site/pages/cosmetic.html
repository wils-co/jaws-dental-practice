Clinical Realities & Risks (AHPRA Section 133)

Elective cosmetic procedures carry clinical implications that must be weighed carefully:

  • Irreversibility of Enamel Preparation: Porcelain veneers require shaving a thin layer of natural enamel. Once prepared, teeth always need coverage.
  • Periodic Maintenance: Whitening requires periodic booster trays; composite resin requires repolishing every 3–5 years; veneers may require remakes in 10–15 years.
  • Night Splint Protection: Clenching or grinding habits (bruxism) will fracture aesthetic ceramic without a custom protective night guard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine whitening and veneers?

Yes. Whitening natural teeth first allows us to match custom veneers to a lighter, brighter baseline shade.

Will cosmetic dentistry look unnatural?

We work exclusively with premier Australian master dental ceramists who hand-layer multi-toned ceramic to replicate natural tooth translucency and light refraction.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We partner with specialized healthcare payment providers (TLC and Afterpay) to help split cosmetic investments into affordable installments.
