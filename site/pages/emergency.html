Common Dental Emergencies We Treat Same-Day

If you are experiencing any of the following symptoms, please contact our clinic immediately:

Urgent

Severe Toothache & Abscess

Throbbing, constant pain keeping you awake, exacerbated by hot or cold liquids, or facial pressure.

Immediate Action: Avoid applying hot compresses directly to the cheek. Take paracetamol/ibuprofen as directed and call us for immediate nerve relief.

Knocked-Out Adult Tooth

Complete avulsion from accidental sports impact, falls, or sudden physical trauma.

Immediate Action: Handle ONLY by the crown. Do not scrub the root. Store in cold milk or in cheek saliva. Re-implantation must occur within 60 minutes.

Chipped or Broken Tooth

Sharp enamel edges cutting the tongue, exposed sensitive yellow dentin, or a fractured cusp.

Immediate Action: Save broken fragments if possible. Avoid chewing on that side. We can smooth sharp margins and bond protective composite.

Lost Crown or Large Filling

Sudden loss of a restoration leaving an open cavity, sharp hole, or intense cold sensitivity.

Immediate Action: Keep the crown clean. Do not attempt superglue. We can disinfect the tooth and recement or seal the space.

What to Expect at an Emergency Visit

Our focus during an urgent appointment is diagnosis and immediate stabilization:

01

Triage Call

Our reception team asks key triage questions and prepares our treatment room for your prompt arrival.

02

Targeted X-Rays

Low-dose digital imaging to inspect root tips, hidden decay, or internal fractures without delay.

03

Immediate Pain Relief

Gentle computerised local numbing to stop acute nerve pain, followed by temporary sealing or pulp extirpation.

04

Definitive Plan

Once you are completely pain-free, Dr. Julia provides a written plan detailing long-term restorative options.

⚠️

When to Go Directly to a Hospital Emergency Department

If your dental infection is accompanied by difficulty swallowing, difficulty breathing, or severe facial swelling closing an eye, this is a medical emergency. Please attend the nearest hospital Emergency Department immediately (such as Monash Medical Centre in Clayton, 246 Clayton Rd) or dial 000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I walk in without calling first?

Calling ahead is strongly advised. Phoning allows us to schedule you into a dedicated buffer slot and have our clinical team prepared the moment you arrive.

Will you pull my tooth today?

Our philosophy is always saving natural teeth whenever clinically possible. Tooth extraction is considered a last resort when a tooth cannot be saved with root canal therapy or bonding.

Can I claim health insurance for emergency visits?

Yes. We have an on-site HICAPS terminal to process instant rebates with all major Australian health funds.
