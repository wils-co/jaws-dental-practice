Your first visit to Smiles by Design

We know visiting a new dentist can cause anxiety. At Smiles by Design in Wheelers Hill, we do not believe in rushed 10-minute exams or scolding patients. Your first appointment is an unrushed, 45-minute comprehensive assessment focused entirely on understanding your oral health goals in a calm environment.

What to Expect During Your 45-Minute Visit

We believe in taking the time to see the whole picture before suggesting any treatment:

01

Listening & Goals

We discuss your dental history, any past uncomfortable experiences, and what you want to achieve for your smile.

02

Thorough Examination

Meticulous check of teeth, gums, bite dynamics, jaw joints, and an oral cancer soft-tissue screening.

03

Digital Imaging

Low-dose digital X-rays and high-resolution intraoral camera photos so you can see exactly what Dr. Julia sees.

04

Clear Written Options

You receive an itemized treatment summary with exact ADA item numbers and fee estimates to take home with zero pressure.

✦

Supporting Anxious & Nervous Patients

Dental anxiety is normal. The plan is a patient-guided pace: you can pause. Specific numbing kit is not advertised until it is in the rooms. Call contact once a phone number is live.
