Early Release of Superannuation on Compassionate Grounds

Under the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) Compassionate Release of Superannuation scheme, individuals experiencing acute oral pain, severe chewing impairment, or missing teeth impacting overall nutrition can apply to release super funds to cover dental care (such as dental implants, All-on-4, or extensive restorative surgery). We supply the required independent dental reports and formal treatment quotes.

Common Australian Dental (ADA) Item Numbers

You can use these item numbers when contacting your private health insurer to check your exact rebate allowances:

  • 012: Comprehensive Oral Examination
  • 022: Intraoral Periapical Radiograph (X-Ray)
  • 114: Removal of Calculus (Dental Scale and Clean)
  • 121: Topical Fluoride Application
  • 531: Adhesive Composite Resin Restoration (Single Surface)
  • 583: Porcelain Veneer (Indirect Ceramic Facing)
  • 688: First Stage Surgical Dental Implant Insertion
  • 672: Full Ceramic Crown on Implant
