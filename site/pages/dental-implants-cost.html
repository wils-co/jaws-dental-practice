Transparent Fee Estimates & Treatment Scope

A dental implant consists of three physical components: the titanium fixture, custom connector (abutment), and the permanent ceramic tooth. Here is how fees compare across single and full-arch solutions:

Most Common

Single Dental Implant

Replaces a single missing tooth root and crown without compromising surrounding teeth.

  • Typical Fee Guide$4,500 – $6,500
  • Diagnostic 3D CBCTItem 026
  • Surgical FixtureItem 688
  • Custom AbutmentItem 661
  • Final Ceramic CrownItem 672

Stage payments split between surgical placement and final crown delivery 3–6 months later.

All-on-4 Full-Arch

A complete fixed bridge replacing an entire upper or lower arch of missing or failing teeth.

  • Typical Fee Guide$22,000 – $32,000+ / arch

Compassionate Release of Superannuation

The Australian Government permits early release of superannuation funds on compassionate grounds for essential medical and dental treatments. If you suffer from acute oral pain, severe chewing dysfunction, or missing teeth impacting overall health, superannuation may fund your treatment:

01

Clinical Assessment

Comprehensive exam and 3D imaging with Dr Julia Nguyen to formally diagnose dental impairment and treatment necessity.

02

Medical Certificates

We provide the required detailed clinical report and quote. A second report is completed by your general medical practitioner (GP).

03

ATO Application

Submit your application securely through your MyGov portal under the ATO Compassionate Release of Superannuation section.

04

Release & Treatment

Upon ATO approval (typically 2–3 weeks), super funds are transferred to your bank account, and your clinical treatment begins.

Government Role Disclosure: Smiles by Design assists with clinical documentation and quotes, but does not approve or guarantee release. Eligibility is determined independently by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).

Private Health Funds & HICAPS

Dental implant benefits depend on your specific health insurer and level of "Major Dental" extras cover. Common item numbers used when checking your rebate include:

  • Item 012: Comprehensive Oral Examination
  • Item 026: Cone Beam Volumetric Tomography (3D CBCT Scan)
  • Item 688: Surgical Placement of First Stage Dental Implant
  • Item 661: Fitting of Implant Abutment
  • Item 672: Full Crown Attached to Osseointegrated Implant

Our reception team can swipe your physical or digital health fund card through our HICAPS terminal to provide an immediate quote on available rebates.

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Fees

Why does implant cost vary so much?

Every jaw is biologically unique. A patient with thick bone may only need an implant and crown, while another patient may require socket preservation, bone grafting, or a sinus lift before an implant can securely hold.

Does Medicare cover dental implants in Australia?

No. In Australia, Medicare does not cover routine dental treatment or dental implants in private dental clinics.

Are consultations free?

No. Dr Julia Nguyen conducts a thorough 45-minute clinical assessment including soft tissue checks, bite dynamics, and radiographic evaluation. We provide professional medical care, not high-pressure sales pitches.

What if an implant fails—do I pay again?

Our written treatment plan clearly specifies guarantees on laboratory porcelain and our clinical revision policy in the rare event of osseointegration failure.
