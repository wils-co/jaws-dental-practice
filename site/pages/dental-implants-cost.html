Dental implants cost

Dental implants cost

Implant fees are a plan, not a sticker on a website. At Smiles by Design in Wheelers Hill, Dr Julia Nguyen will write down what you actually need after an exam. This page is how that number is built, and what is not included until we say it is.

Nothing here is a quote.

Why there isn’t a single price

A “dental implant” in conversation often means three things: the fixture in the bone, the connector (abutment), and the crown. People also say it when they mean a full-arch bridge. Those are different treatments.

The fee changes with:

  • How many teeth are being replaced, and in which jaw
  • Whether a tooth still has to come out, and whether you wait for healing or go toward immediate placement
  • Whether the gums need treatment first
  • Whether extra bone or gum tissue is needed
  • Whose surgery it is (this practice, or a referred colleague)
  • What sits on top: a single crown, a bridge, a denture on implants, or All-on-4
  • Lab work, extra scans, and how many review visits are in the plan
  • Whether something already in the mouth (a denture, a neighbouring crown) has to be remade so the implant can work

Two people with “one missing tooth” can leave with two different plans. That is normal.

What we will put in writing

After Julia has looked, you should leave with:

  • The clinical plan, in plain language
  • What is included in the figure (surgery, parts, crown, reviews for a named period)
  • What is not included (grafts, a night splint, treating other teeth, remakes after a named period, the other jaw)
  • Item numbers, so you can ask your health fund
  • The sequence of visits and which fees are due when

If that pack is missing, ask for it before you book surgery.

Guide figures (when we have them)

We will publish a small guide on this page once Julia has set it: an exam, a single-implant pathway with a straightforward crown, and what a graft or an extraction adds as a separate line.

Until those numbers are on the page, please do not treat any implant price you saw on another clinic’s site, on social media, or in a Facebook ad as ours.

All-on-4 will never share a single-tooth guide figure. If you need a full arch, that is its own written plan.

Health funds and payment

Bring your card. Reception can check rebate estimates against item numbers. An estimate from a fund is not our invoice, and it is not approval of the treatment.

Payment plans, if we offer them, are a way to pay a plan you have already agreed. They are not a reason to say yes. Third-party finance has its own contract and its own no. We will not write that approval is guaranteed.

Superannuation on compassionate grounds is a separate, regulated process. The clinic does not approve releases. Ask us only if the treatment in your plan is the kind of work that process is even about, and read the official rules. It is a last-resort conversation, not a sales path.

How we talk about “cheap” and “from”

A weekly “from” price on an implant page usually hides the crown, the graft, or the second jaw. We won’t use that pattern.

A figure that is only available if you attend a free consult will not appear here either. The first visit is an exam unless we booked something else. Your first visit

FAQs

How much are dental implants?

It depends on how many teeth, whether a graft or extraction is needed, whose surgery it is, and what sits on top. We will publish a small guide on this page when Julia has set those figures. Until then, the only honest number is the one in your written plan after an exam.

How much do dental implants cost?

Same answer as above. “Dental implants cost” in a search usually means one tooth plus the crown. A full arch is All-on-4, which is a different plan.

How much are dental implants in Australia?

Australian fees vary by city, by surgeon, and by what’s included. A figure from another clinic’s ad is not ours. Guide-only numbers, when we have them, will be listed above. They are not a quote for Melbourne or for Wheelers Hill until they are in your plan.

Can I use my super for dental implants?

Sometimes people apply for early release of superannuation on compassionate grounds. That is a government process. We do not approve or refuse it. We can provide clinical records and a treatment plan if you ask, and only if the work in that plan is the kind of treatment the scheme is even about. Read the ATO / Department of Human Services rules yourself. It is a last-resort pathway, not a payment plan we sell.

Are dental implants covered by health insurance?

Sometimes part of the exam, X-rays, or the crown. Surgical items, waiting periods, and annual limits vary. A fund estimate is not our invoice and is not approval of the treatment. Medicare does not generally cover dental implants in a private clinic. Bring your card; reception can check rebate estimates against item numbers.

Can you tell me the cost on the phone?

We can tell you how we structure fees, and whether you are asking about one tooth or a full arch. We cannot quote your mouth without seeing it.

Do you bulk bill implants?

No. This practice does not bulk bill.

What if the implant fails?

Failure is a real risk. What we would do next (replace, wait, change plan) and what is covered in the original fee should be in your written plan. Ask that question before surgery, not after.
