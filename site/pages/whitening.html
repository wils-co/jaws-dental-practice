The Professional Whitening Process

We ensure teeth are clinically clean and gums are protected before applying active peroxide:

01

Pre-Bleach Clean

We polish away surface tartar and stains so whitening gel contacts natural enamel directly and evenly.

02

Gingival Barrier

A protective liquid resin dam is applied along your gumline to prevent gel from touching sensitive soft tissues.

03

Peroxide Application

Medical-grade gel applied in 15-minute cycles, activating oxygen molecules to dissolve deep internal stains.

04

Remineralising Finish

A soothing desensitising mineral treatment applied to lock in shade, hydrate enamel, and stop zingers.

✦

Managing Tooth Sensitivity & The "White Diet"

Enamel pores remain microscopically open for 48 hours following professional whitening. We recommend sticking to a "white diet" (water, milk, white rice, chicken, bananas) and avoiding dark pigments like black coffee, soy sauce, curry, and red wine during this initial window.

Clinical Limitations & Facts (AHPRA Section 133)

Whitening is a safe cosmetic treatment, but realistic biological limits apply:

  • Restorations Do Not Change Shade: Dental crowns, fillings, and porcelain veneers will NOT lighten with bleaching. If you have front fillings, they may need updating after whitening.
  • Temporary Tooth Zingers: Mild cold sensitivity or electric sensations ("zingers") are common for 24 hours. They are temporary and resolve completely.
  • Natural Genetic Tone: Everyone has a natural genetic ceiling for tooth shade based on dentin thickness. We aim for healthy, natural brilliance rather than fake chalky white.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is dental whitening better than supermarket kits?

Over-the-counter kits are restricted by law to less than 6% hydrogen peroxide, so they work slowly and give modest results. Supermarket charcoal powders are also highly abrasive and can scratch enamel over time.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have gum recession?

Yes, but exposed tooth roots (dentin) are more sensitive to peroxide. In our clinic, Dr Julia coats exposed roots with protective resin barriers before applying gel.

Does teeth whitening damage tooth enamel?

No. When professionally formulated at a neutral pH, peroxide works through the enamel surface to break down pigmented stains without eroding minerals.
