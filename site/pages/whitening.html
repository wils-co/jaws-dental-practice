Professional teeth whitening

Professional teeth whitening

Whitening can lighten natural teeth. It does not change crowns, veneers, or fillings. Results vary, sensitivity is common, and it is not permanent. At Smiles by Design in Wheelers Hill, Dr Julia Nguyen will check your teeth and gums before anyone puts gel on them.

What it is

Most dental whitening uses a peroxide gel in trays (take-home) or in a clinic visit. The gel can lighten the colour of enamel and dentine. How much it lightens depends on the starting colour, the cause of the stain, and how your teeth respond. Yellowing from age often responds more than grey or mottled colour. Tetracycline staining and white spots are a different conversation.

Over-the-counter strips and charcoal pastes are not the same as a planned gel in custom trays. We won’t pretend they are identical, and we also won’t scare you with “never use a shop product” as a sales line. If you’ve already used something, say so.

Who it may suit

This may be worth discussing if:

  • Your natural teeth have yellowed and you want them lighter
  • Gums are healthy enough, or can be, before gel sits against them
  • You understand existing restorations will not change colour, and may then look more obvious

It may be a poor fit if you are very sensitive already, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding (we’ll delay), if decay or leaking work needs to be dealt with first, or if the teeth you care about are mostly porcelain.

Whitening is a poor substitute for veneers when the issue is shape, and a poor substitute for aligners when the issue is position.

What happens

  1. Look first. Exam, and a clean if the surface stain would get in the way. We will not bleach around gum disease or holes.
  2. Choose a method. Take-home trays, an in-clinic session, or a mix. Julia will say what she actually offers, not a menu we cannot deliver.
  3. Written plan. Which teeth, which method, how to use it, sensitivity, and a guide cost. Not a shade number promised on a card.
  4. Treatment. Gel on the teeth for the time in the plan. More is not better. Leaving gel longer than instructed is how you get burns and a worse week.
  5. Review. Colour, sensitivity, and whether a restoration now looks wrong next to the tooth.

Where this is done: At this practice. Take-home trays are worn at home after they are made. There is no “unlimited whitening for life” membership on this page.

Risks and recovery

Whitening is one of the gentler cosmetic options. It still has risks:

  • Sensitivity to cold, sometimes sharp, usually settling after days or weeks, not always
  • White blotches while you treat, which often even out, and sometimes don’t
  • Soft-tissue burns if gel sits on gums or lips
  • Uneven colour, especially next to restorations
  • Relapse: colour drifts back, faster with smoking and staining drinks
  • Rarely, a tooth that was already dying becomes obviously darker or painful and needs other treatment

If you have severe pain, gum burns, or a tooth that goes dark or throbs, stop and call.

Other options

  • Do nothing
  • A clean, if the stain is on the surface
  • Bonding or veneers if colour will not shift, knowing those are more destructive
  • Replacing old yellowed restorations, which is a different fee

Aftercare

Avoid heavy staining food and drink for a short window if that was in your instructions. Use the toothpaste you were given if sensitivity is an issue. Don’t top up with extra gel “for luck”. Repeat treatments, if any, wait until Julia says the teeth can take it.

FAQs

Does it hurt?

Often a bit. Sometimes a lot, briefly. We don’t advertise this as pain-free. We can change strength, time, or stop.

How white will I go?

To a lighter version of your teeth, not to a magazine file. We will not pick a shade card as a guarantee.

How long does it last?

Months to a few years of a noticeable difference is a fair way to think, not a warranty. Habits matter.

How much does teeth whitening cost?

In-clinic professional whitening and take-home trays are different fees. Guide figures will sit on Fees when Julia has set them. A plan for you still follows an exam. We do not bulk bill. This page is about treatment in the practice, not DIY strips or charcoal. Shop kits are cheaper and less controlled; that is a real trade, not a trick question.
