Porcelain vs. Composite: The Honest Comparison

A veneer is not a single product. Porcelain and composite are fundamentally different in longevity, tooth preparation, and investment. Here is how they compare:

Longest Lasting

Porcelain Veneers

Handcrafted laboratory ceramics made to match natural tooth translucency and light reflection.

  • Lifespan10–15+ Years
  • Enamel Preparation0.3–0.7 mm (Irreversible)
  • Appointments2 Visits (Prep & Fit)
  • Stain ResistanceVirtually Stain-Proof
  • Best ForDurability & Complete Makeovers

Crafted with local Australian dental laboratories, never cheap overseas imports.

Composite Veneers

Medical resin sculpted directly onto the tooth surface in the dental chair in a single appointment.

  • Lifespan4–7 Years
  • Enamel PreparationMinimal to None
  • AppointmentsSingle Visit (Same-day)
  • Stain ResistanceModerate (Needs periodic polish)
  • Best ForSingle Chips, Minor Gaps & Budget

Easily repaired or modified chairside if a small chip ever occurs.

Who Veneers May Suit

This may be worth discussing if:

  • You have local colour, shape, or chip issues that whitening or a small bond won’t solve
  • The teeth in question have enough sound natural structure
  • You understand future repairs, replacements, and the way gums age naturally around a facing

It may be a poor fit if you want a “full new smile” as a generic commodity, if your bite is heavy and likely to snap facings, if gums are inflamed, or if you grind and won't wear a night splint. Heavily restored teeth often require crowns instead.

Honest Clinical Considerations & Risks

Veneers are not temporary cosmetics; they require lifelong maintenance. Under Australian health law (AHPRA s133), every surgical and cosmetic dental procedure carries risks that you should know before deciding:

  • Irreversible Enamel Removal: Enamel does not grow back. If a veneer chips or wears down in 12 years, that tooth will always need a restoration.
  • Temperature Sensitivity: Cold sensitivity is common during the first 1–2 weeks following tooth preparation.
  • Bite Wear & Chipping: Grinding habits (bruxism) require a custom night splint to protect ceramic margins.
  • Gum Margins: Natural gum lines recede gradually over decades, which may eventually expose the edge of the facing.

Conservative Alternatives to Consider

  • Professional Whitening — If natural tooth shape is already pleasing and colour is the only concern.
  • Invisalign Clear Aligners — If crowding or rotation is the real issue; straightening roots is often far better than shaving down enamel.
  • Composite Bonding — Highly conservative, repairable chairside, but picks up staining faster over time.
  • Do Nothing — Healthy natural teeth are always precious. Waiting is a valid decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does getting veneers hurt?

Preparation is carried out with computer-assisted local anaesthetic so you shouldn't feel pain during the appointment. Afterwards, mild temperature sensitivity is common for a few days while gums settle. We do not call this pain-free, but it is manageable with mild over-the-counter pain relief.

How much do veneers cost in Melbourne?

Veneer fees depend on whether you select handcrafted porcelain or direct composite, how many teeth are treated, and whether diagnostic mock-ups or night splints are included. A written, itemized fee plan is provided after your comprehensive exam. We do not bulk bill.

How long do veneers realistically last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15+ years with proper oral hygiene and routine check-ups. Composite veneers typically last 4 to 7 years before needing repolishing or replacement. Grinding, biting hard objects, and gum health directly influence lifespan.

Can I see before and after photos?

Under AHPRA advertising guidelines, outcome galleries that imply a guaranteed result are prohibited. During your private consultation, Dr. Julia can walk you through individual clinical case studies demonstrating realistic anatomical outcomes with accompanying clinical risks.
