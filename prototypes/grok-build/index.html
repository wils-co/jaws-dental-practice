The approach

A dentist can only sit in one chair. So the practice is built around the plan being Julia's, and the day-to-day sitting with a small team she trusts.

Assessment first

Nothing is quoted or promised before an examination. Suitability is something Julia assesses in the chair, not something a website decides.

Honest referral

Where treatment is better placed elsewhere, we say so on the page for that treatment and refer.

Risks stated

Every cosmetic and invasive treatment page carries its risks and recovery, and there is a treatment risks page in the footer.

Fees

Published as a guide, not a quote.

Indicative ranges are published for each treatment so you can plan. What your own treatment costs depends on what the examination finds.

You will have a written plan with itemised fees before anything begins. Health fund item numbers are included so you can check your cover.

Next step

Start with an assessment.

One appointment to look properly, talk through what is realistic, and put a written plan in front of you. No treatment is booked on the day unless you want it to be.
