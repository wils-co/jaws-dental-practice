**H1:** Cosmetic dentistry

## What we mean by cosmetic

People say “cosmetic” when they mean several different jobs:

- Colour: usually [whitening](/whitening), not veneers first
- Shape or a chip: bonding or [veneers](/veneers)
- Position: [aligners](/invisalign) (or braces), not a row of facings on crooked teeth
- A missing tooth: [an implant](/implants), a bridge, or a denture
- A mouth that is failing more widely: a rebuild that may include [All-on-4](/all-on-4), which is rehabilitation, not a polish

The right page is the one that matches the problem. If you are not sure, book an exam and say what you see in the mirror. Julia will name the smallest treatment that could work, and the one that would be hard to undo.

## Who it may suit

This may be worth discussing if something about your teeth is bothering you and you want options, with risks, before anyone prepares a tooth.

It may be a poor fit if you want a guaranteed look from a photo, if gums and decay haven’t been dealt with, or if you are hoping a website package will replace a diagnosis.

## What happens

1. **Say the thing that bothers you.** One tooth, several, colour, a gap, a chip.
2. **Exam.** The same look we would give any new patient: teeth, gums, bite, existing work. Photos when they help. X-rays when they change the plan.
3. **A list, not a bundle.** What we could do, what we shouldn’t, what can wait, and what would be referred. Cost as a guide, in writing, for the path you actually choose.
4. **Time.** Whitening is not veneers. Veneers are not aligners. You don’t have to decide in the chair.

**Where this is done:** At this practice, with Julia, except where a page already says a stage may be referred (implants, full-arch surgery, some ortho). Injectables (anti-wrinkle, fillers) are a later question for this practice. They are not offered on launch and they are not a dental exam.

## FAQs

**What is cosmetic dentistry?**
Here it means colour, shape, chips, gaps, or replacing a missing front tooth. The work still has to function. Cost, duration, and risks live on the specific pages: [veneers](/veneers), [teeth whitening](/whitening), [Invisalign](/invisalign), [dental implants](/implants). This hub is the map, not a package.

**Can I book a free cosmetic consult?**
No. The first visit is an exam unless we booked emergency care. That’s the honest version of “come in and talk”.

**Will I see a smile gallery?**
Not as a menu of typical outcomes. Individual cases, if Julia later supplies them, will be labelled as one person, with risks next to them.

**Do you do the whole face, not just teeth?**
Not at launch. Teeth and gums first.
