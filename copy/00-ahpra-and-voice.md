# Voice and AHPRA rules (Julia’s first practice)

## Voice

Julia’s voice SSOT is `copy/julia-approved-2026-08/voice-guide.md` (approved 9 Aug 2026). Check drafts against that file. Short version: measured, professional, warm without gushing. “Less fluffy” means not saccharine, not terse. Specific and checkable over adjectives. Third person for bio.

We / the practice on service pages. Julia by name on Home and About. Do not invent a house-brand process name. Bio text is `bio-FINAL.md` — do not re-edit without her. Degree is BDSc **UWA 2009**, not Melbourne. Never prosthodontist / specialist / specialising in. Never “experienced implant surgeon.”

Do **not** promise “you always see Julia.” A dentist’s diary does not scale; the claim becomes false the day the practice hires. Principal-led means she owns the plan and the complex work. Other visits can sit with a small team she trusts.

## Never

- Outcome testimonials, including Google quotes about clinical results, “excellent work”, or how a smile turned out
- Best, leading, #1, world-class, luxury, gold standard, guaranteed, pain-free as a promise, only dentist you’ll need
- Before-and-after galleries that imply a typical result (no Results in the nav)
- Award walls, patient/implant/veneer counters, “largest in Australia”
- Specialist titles unless Julia is registered in that specialty
- In-house GA, CEREC, 15 dentists, or any kit/team we have not confirmed
- Product schema with star ratings on treatment URLs
- Complimentary consult copy that sounds like a free diagnosis or a promised smile

## Always

- AHPRA registration number on About
- Risks and recovery on every cosmetic or invasive treatment page, plus a footer Treatment risks page
- Fees described as a guide; a plan only after examination
- Honest in-house vs referred. If Julia refers implants, All-on-4, or Invisalign, say so on that page
- Suitability: “Julia will assess” not “you are a candidate”
- One primary CTA (Book) and the phone number

## Before/after, later

Only if Julia supplies real cases, comparable photos, individual-results language, and the risks sit next to the images. Not day one.
